# SIDE QUEST CLUB - Vision & Mission

> Every stranger is a side quest you haven't started yet.

## Vision
A world where meeting someone new only takes one small push.

## Mission
We give people the first push. Two things make a night unforgettable: stepping out of your comfort zone, and the people you meet when you do. The hard part is always the first ten seconds. So we hand people the opener: a side quest, a small adventure to do together. By the time it's done, they're not strangers any more. The rest is the easy part.

## What we believe
- Getting out of your comfort zone is easier with an excuse. We are the excuse.
- You don't get to know people by talking about the weather. You get to know them by doing something together.
- The opener is the hard part; everything after it is easy.
- It should take one scan and zero apps.
- Quests are done together, never at someone's expense.

## Where it comes from
Aron has always loved going out and meeting new people, in clubs and in any social situation. He is a very extroverted person. He first made a shirt just for himself, with a QR code on the back, so anyone could scan it and get a side quest to do with him. He then realised many other people would want the same thing, and started building Side Quest Club. The project is in its initial stage.

## Where it's going
1. Shirts with a personal QR code, so anyone can be the person who starts the side quest.
2. Shirtless Pass: no shirt needed. Friends each get side quests for the night, go up to strangers themselves, and compare who did the most.
3. Squad packs, and quest packs for different nights (Erasmus, Spicy, Festival, city packs).

## Future: photo and video proof (not built)
If people can upload proof of a completed quest, every photo needs a report button, and reported photos are hidden until reviewed.
4. A community of people who say yes to side quests, starting in Madrid.
