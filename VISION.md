# SIDE QUEST CLUB — Vision & Mission

## Vision
A world where talking to a stranger is the easy part of the night.

## Mission
Get people out of their comfort zone and into each other's stories. A side quest is a tiny excuse to do something with someone you didn't know five minutes ago. Enough of them, and you get a community of outgoing people who actually know each other.

## What we believe
- The best nights come from the people you didn't plan to meet.
- Most people want to connect; they just need an excuse. We make the excuse.
- It should take one scan and zero apps.
- Quests are done together, never at someone's expense.
- The shirt should be something you'd wear anyway.

## Where it comes from
Aron has always loved going out and meeting new people, in clubs and in any social situation. He is a very extroverted person. He first made a shirt just for himself, with a QR code on the back, so anyone could scan it and get a side quest to do with him. He then realised many other people would want the same thing, and started building Side Quest Club. The project is in its initial stage.

## Where it's going
1. Shirts with a personal QR code, so anyone can be the person who starts the side quest.
2. A way to play without a shirt (Shirtless Pass).
3. Group packs and new quest decks.
4. A real community of people who say yes to side quests, starting in Madrid.
